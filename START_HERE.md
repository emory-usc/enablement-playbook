# Start Here

The entry point for the enablement kit. This tells you which prompts to apply,
lists the required inputs, and restates the guardrails.

## The three workflows

1. **Learn / master a product (whitepaper)** — use when you need to research,
   prepare, or build a confident point of view.
2. **Create a customer deck** — use when you need a customer-facing
   presentation or executive briefing.
3. **Generate both (whitepaper → deck)** — product mastery *and* a customer
   deck for the same product.

## Always applied first

- `prompts/00_shared_foundation.md` — audience, principles, tone, guardrails.
- `prompts/03_orchestrator_prompt.md` — routes the request to the right prompt.

## Required inputs

Capture these before generating.

- Product (name, category, version)
- Customer name
- Industry / segment
- Customer business priority
- Known customer pain points
- Known technical environment
- Known risks or blockers
- Intended meeting type (discovery, executive briefing, architecture
  discussion, demo prep, POC proposal, or workshop)
- Audience seniority (executive, technical decision maker, architect,
  security/risk/compliance, or mixed)
- Desired next step
- Source materials available

Missing something? Don't guess — record it under **Questions to Validate** and
mark it as an assumption.

## Guardrails (restated)

- No source, no strong claim.
- No customer relevance, no live slide.
- Do not invent customer details.
- Do not overpromise.
- Mark assumptions clearly.
- Every claim carries a status.
