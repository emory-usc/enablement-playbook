# Shared Foundation

Applies to every artifact this kit produces. Load this first, before any other
prompt.

## Audience

Enterprise technical buyers and their teams: executives, architects,
security/risk/compliance, and technical decision makers. They are time-poor and
skeptical. Every artifact must answer "why should I care?" before "how does it
work?".

## Principles

1. **Business value first.** Lead with the customer outcome, not the feature.
2. **Technically credible.** Every strong claim traces to a source.
3. **Risk aware.** Call out compliance, security, governance, and operational
   implications even when the product is good.
4. **Concise.** Enough for a decision, not a product dump.

## Tone

Direct, specific, and unpretentious. Prefer short sentences. Avoid
vendor-speak, superlatives, and unverifiable adjectives ("best-in-class",
"industry-leading").

## Guardrails

- No source, no strong claim.
- No customer relevance, no live slide.
- Do not invent customer details.
- Do not overpromise compliance, security, availability, performance,
  licensing, regional availability, private networking, roadmap, or
  integration support.
- Mark assumptions clearly and route unknowns to *Questions to Validate*.
- Every claim carries a status: `Confirmed`, `Needs validation`, or
  `Do not present`.
